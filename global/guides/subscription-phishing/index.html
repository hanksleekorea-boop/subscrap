Subscription review guide

Recognize subscription cancellation and renewal phishing

Urgent renewal messages often imitate known services; verify them without following embedded login or payment links.

Review checklist

  1. Open the service from a saved bookmark or type its known official domain instead of using the message link.
  2. Check sender domain, order history and the official account page before entering credentials or card data.
  3. Report suspicious evidence separately and never classify it as a confirmed subscription charge.
