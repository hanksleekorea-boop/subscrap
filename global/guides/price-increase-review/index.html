Subscription review guide

Detect a subscription price change without overreacting to one charge

A changed amount may be a price increase, tax, currency movement, plan change or one-time purchase.

Advertising boundary: ads may appear only on this public page after consent. Personal subscription data from the app is never sent to advertising systems.

Review checklist

  1. Compare the same merchant, product, billing cycle and original currency across periods.
  2. Look for an official price-change notice and identify whether tax or a different tier explains the amount.
  3. Present a review alert before changing the saved recurring amount or predicting future cash flow.

Scope

General guidance for reviewing your own subscriptions. Act only as the account owner or an authorized administrator.

Before you act

Results are candidates within the sources and dates you supplied. No result does not mean no paid service. Match the payment date, amount and account before confirming.
