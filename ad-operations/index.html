Automatic stops

Privacy boundary violations, policy warnings and consent errors stop all advertising. Supply-chain mismatch, invalid traffic, errors, latency, layout shift, stale metrics or a sampled revenue collapse stop external supply and fall back to house or no ads.

Quarterly privacy, advertising-policy and country-law reviews are mandatory. These limits are safety controls, not revenue promises.
