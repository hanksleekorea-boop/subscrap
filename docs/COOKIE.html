쿠키·기기 저장소 정책 / Cookie & Device Storage Policy

광고 관련 저장과 분석은 기본 거부입니다. 개인 앱의 필수 저장소와 공개 콘텐츠의 광고 저장소를 분리합니다.

기본 거부 / Default denied

Ad storage, ad user data, ad personalization and analytics storage start denied. A refusal never blocks the free private app.

개인 앱 / Private app

Gmail, receipts, transaction tables, discovered subscriptions, amounts, renewal dates and account identifiers are never used for advertising cookies or measurement.

공개 콘텐츠 / Public content

Only the public guide surface can connect to advertising. In consent-required regions, a Google-certified CMP must provide a valid signal before the loader can run.

선택과 삭제 / Choices and deletion

The Privacy choices control reopens the certified CMP. Browser storage can also be removed in browser settings; personal app data is managed through the app export and deletion tools.

현재 광고 송출은 비활성화되어 있습니다. 실제 사업자와 보관기간은 운영 계정 검증 뒤 확정합니다. Advertising is currently disabled; provider-specific details will be added only after account verification.
