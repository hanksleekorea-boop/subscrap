광고 정책 / Advertising Policy

광고는 공개 가이드에서만 허용될 수 있으며 개인 구독 앱은 언제나 무광고입니다.

허용 표면 / Allowed surface

Only public educational guides can contain ad placements. Login, Gmail, receipt, transaction, discovery, review, export and deletion screens are prohibited advertising surfaces.

금지 데이터 / Prohibited data

Gmail, receipts, payment files, service names, amounts, renewal dates and user decisions are not shared for ad selection, personalization, measurement or profiling.

동의 / Consent

Consent defaults to denied. Where consent is required, only a valid signal from a Google-certified CMP can activate the public-page ad loader.

개통 조건 / Activation gates

Ads remain disabled until the account, site, publisher ID, certified CMP, custom HTTPS domain, tax, payment, legal review, content review and ads.txt evidence are all verified.

품질 / Quality

SubScrap prohibits self-clicks, automated clicks, deceptive labels, accidental-click layouts and ads near privacy or security controls.

Google AdSense 코드와 광고 단위 4종, Google 인증 CMP가 공개 가이드에 설치되어 있습니다. 현재 Google 사이트 심사가 준비 중이므로 사이트가 준비됨으로 승인될 때까지 광고 노출은 보장되지 않습니다. AdSense code, four ad-unit formats, and Google's certified CMP are installed on public guides; ad fill is not guaranteed until Google marks the site Ready.
